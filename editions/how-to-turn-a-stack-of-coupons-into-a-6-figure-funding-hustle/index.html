How to Turn a Stack of Coupons Into a 6-Figure Funding Hustle

Summarizes a training session on finding local prospects through coupon advertisers, targeted industries, and direct business outreach.

Jason Feimster · Moonshine Capital

ORIGINAL LINKEDIN EDITION ↗
Originally published 2025-05-21. Historical offers, program terms, and results reflect the original article and may have changed. Some original illustrations are linked rather than hosted. Moonshine Capital may receive affiliate compensation; no funding or income outcome is guaranteed.

Based on DAC’s Agent Training, May 21, 2025 — FeaturingBank Breezy CEO, Wade Cordell 🔗 Watch the full replay on YouTube

Let me be real with you. You don’t need a fancy funnel. You don’t need to memorize some pitch. You don’t even need to know how to spell “underwriting.” You just need hustle, heart—and maybe a damn coupon pack.

Welcome to Wednesday with Wade—a training session that wasn’t just another webinar. It was a how-to-clinic for how to make real money helping small businesses who’ve been ghosted by their banks and ignored by traditional lenders. If you’re trying to build a DAC agency with Moonshine Capital and haven’t internalized what Wade Cordell just laid out… you’re out here playing business while others are getting paid.

So here’s your punch-you-in-the-throat recap, with no fluff, no fluffing, and nothing held back.

View original article illustration ↗

🧨 Why You’re Broke (and What to Do About It)

Let’s cut the crap: Most agents aren’t broke because the system doesn’t work. They’re broke because they refuse to work the damn system.

Wade opened the session reminding us of the gold mine we’re standing on. DAC and Bank Breezy together built an automated online application platform with 80% approval rates for small business working capital. That’s the #1 tool business owners need to survive and scale.

💸 "Every time someone gets funded, you get paid." —Wade Cordell

We’re not competing with our agents. We’re not gatekeeping. We’re building a distribution army—and you’re the infantry. Get 12–24 fundings under your belt, and watch clients boomerang back to you 4 to 6 times for more.

If you’re not seeing repeat fundings, it’s not the clients. It’s you. You never followed up.

💼 Deals Are Everywhere. Especially In Your Trash Can.

Let’s talk tactics.

Wade held up a Valpak mailer—you know, the junk mail you toss while eating cereal—and said: This is a stack of leads.

While most people see spam, Wade sees 36–38 local small businesses trying to get customers. Lawn care, HVAC, restaurants—they’re already spending money on marketing. Which means they have a reason to want more working capital.

🔥 "I call them. I ask if they serve my area. And then I flip the convo." —Wade Cordell

Script: "Hey, I got your Valpak offer. Quick question—do you also serve folks in XYZ? I’ve got friends there who’d love this."

Pause. They respond.

Then: "Cool, by the way, I work with small business owners like you. I’m the local funding director here with David Allen Capital. If you ever have a great idea you need to act on fast—or you just need help making payroll—we’ve got a funding platform with no credit pull and 24-hour approvals. Can I shoot you some info in case you or someone you know ever needs it?"

Boom. Conversation started. Permission granted. Now they’re a warm lead.

Most of you are over here building funnels in Canva, while Wade is printing money from Valpak.

View original article illustration ↗

🧠 How Smart Agents Think (Hint: It’s Not "How Can I Sell?"...)

Want to win in this business? Stop selling. Start sorting.

DAC’s funding model doesn’t require persuasion—it rewards positioning. You’re not a salesman. You’re a matchmaker. You introduce small business owners to solutions that solve real cash flow problems.

And guess what? The funding world doesn’t operate like mortgages or credit cards. DAC prioritizes revenue over credit. That means a restaurant with tax liens can still get $120K approved in 13 hours.

If you think you need a 700 credit score to help someone? You’re still thinking like a bank. And banks are only helping 10% of business owners.

We’re here for the 90% who get declined. And those 90% are the most underserved, overworked, and overlooked people in the economy.

Sound like your people? Then keep reading.

View original article illustration ↗

📞 Follow-Up Isn’t Optional. It’s the Job.

Wade pulled back the curtain on what really happens after a client fills out the Bank Breezy application:

  • They (and you) get email confirmations.
  • If they stall out mid-app, the team calls them.
  • If they can’t upload bank statements, someone walks them through it.
  • Once offers come in, you’re notified immediately.

What’s your job? Follow up. Text them. Call them. Reaffirm that someone's working on their behalf. Don’t leave them dangling like every other scammy online lender.

Your lead isn’t just “waiting to hear back.” They’re being actively supported by a whole backend team—but it’s your job to stay plugged in and encourage completion.

🧠 Industry Intel – Where the Gold Is Buried

During Q&A, Wade dropped insider tips on which industries are goldmines for DAC agents:

  • Home Healthcare → Big payrolls, slow government payments.
  • Construction → Fast funding needed for inventory and bids.
  • Funeral Homes → (Yeah, seriously) High margins, constant cash needs.
  • Restaurants → Tax issues? Doesn't matter. They need liquidity.

Oh, and Giggle only does a hard pull after approval—not during app. So stop letting the "hard pull" excuse scare you or your client off.

View original article illustration ↗

🙅♂️ Bonus: Can I Fund Myself and Get Paid?

Short answer? Not unless your enroller approves it.

DAC protects the person who referred the agent. You can’t sign up, fund yourself, and cut out your upline. That ain’t how we roll.

But if your enroller gives the greenlight? You might get paid. It’s a trust system—and you don’t start a business by scamming your way through the front door.

🚀 Final Word: Stop Waiting. Start Calling.

If you’re still stuck in analysis paralysis, trying to figure out how to “get started”—stop it. Print out a list of local businesses. Hit up Valpak. Walk your block. Send 20 texts.

“You don’t need everyone. You just need the right ones.” —Wade Cordell

In this business, money rewards momentum. Get off your ass and go make something happen.

You’ve got a platform. A proven system. A no-credit-pull offer. All that’s missing is your activity.

And if that sounds harsh? Good. You needed to hear it.
