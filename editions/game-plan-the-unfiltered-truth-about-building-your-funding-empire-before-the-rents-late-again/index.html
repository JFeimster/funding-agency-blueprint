🔥 Game Plan: The Unfiltered Truth About Building Your Funding Empire (Before the Rent’s Late Again) 🔥

Translates an agent training session into an activity-focused plan for starting a funding business and connecting with potential customers.

Jason Feimster · Moonshine Capital

[Buckle up, this one’s not for the “manifest-it” crowd.]

🎥 Watch the full video training here →

Let me ask you something: Are you gonna build a business, or are you gonna build excuses?

Because if you’re like most of us—scraping together client invoices, side-hustling through marketplace algorithms, dodging self-doubt like it’s your part-time job—then you already know the stakes.

You don’t need another TED Talk on "believing in yourself." You need a damn game plan—something that actually pulls you out of the entrepreneurial hamster wheel and drops you into a system that scales, sustains, and pays your bills before your back breaks from carrying it all alone.

Welcome to Moonshine Capital’s Game Plan, the real-deal roadmap for launching your own funding agency by partnering with David Allen Capital. No MBA required. No trust fund necessary. Just guts, grit, and enough common sense to know that the system is already rigged—and it ain’t rigged in your favor unless you flip the damn board.

🧠 Step 1: Believe in the Thing—Or GTFO

“You have to believe in it. So as long as you believe in it, then you'll talk to people. If you don't believe in it, you won't talk to people.”

Let’s keep it 💯: If you don’t believe in what you’re selling, you’re just another walking spam email in someone’s inbox. You might as well sell expired gym memberships or invite your cousins to another crypto Zoom.

But if you do believe—if you’ve actually sat down, watched the biz op overview, understood how Moonshine Capital connects real business owners to real funding with real urgency—then you owe it to yourself to stop hiding behind “strategy sessions” and “personal brand audits.”

Start talking to people. Like, real people. Not your LinkedIn circle jerk. Not your fake “500 friends” on Facebook. Your warm market. Your neighbor. Your old boss. Your cousin’s ex who started a food truck.

Moonshine Capital isn’t some shiny "side hustle." It’s a damn weapon for people drowning in the financial quicksand of entrepreneurship.

And guess what?

Small biz owners need cash more than they need your six-step Instagram funnel. So why aren't you talking to them yet?

🔎 Step 2: Prescreen Like Your Life Depends On It (Because Maybe It Does)

“So prescreen 'em, prescreen.”

If you’ve ever wasted an hour on a sales call with a "Maybe later" tire-kicker, you know the pain.

Prescreening is your filter for the fluff. Your way to weed out the ‘meh’ and find the ‘move now’. Because this isn’t Girl Scout cookie season. You’re not asking folks if they’re “interested in learning more.” You’re qualifying potential partners, clients, and agents like a shark in open water.

You're not hoping they’re serious. You're making damn sure they are.

Prescreening is your protection from burnout. It’s how you avoid sending 72 texts and getting ghosted by everyone but your aunt Carol.

You’re not desperate. You’re a damn funding closer. Act like it.

📊 Step 3: The Math is the Math—Don’t Fight It

How many people are you gonna talk to? How many agents are you gonna get in this week?

Let’s rip the Band-Aid off right now: Hope is not a business plan.

Moonshine Capital gives you a system, but you still gotta run the plays.

You wanna enroll 5 agents this week? Cool. That means 15 people need to actually watch the biz op overview. Which means you better be reaching out to 25–30 people.

Not staring at Canva. Not journaling your “intentions.” Not tweaking your website's font size for the 7th time.

You’re not some mystical energy healer channeling “abundance.” You’re a business owner, and your results are tied directly to your activity.

Numbers don’t lie. Your feelings might.

You don't need motivation. You need momentum. And momentum starts when you realize this ain't a lottery ticket—it's a lever. The harder you pull, the higher you climb.

💬 Step 4: Use the Damn Scripts

Reach out to 'em, we'll give you the script and text to use.

You know what’s worse than rejection? Winging it.

Moonshine Capital doesn’t hand you vague platitudes and hope you freestyle your way to greatness. We hand you scripts that work, messages that convert, and step-by-step outreach that takes the guesswork out of the grind.

We’re not trying to make you “salesy.” We’re trying to make you effective.

Because you don’t need to “find your voice” in your outreach. You need to use the voice that already prints checks—ours.

💥 Real Talk for the Builders, Hustlers & Bare-Knuckle Dreamers

Look, I know what it’s like. You’ve tried launches. Funnels. TikTok hacks. High-ticket coaching. You’ve bought courses that promised six figures and delivered six headaches. You’ve been told to niche down, charge more, hustle harder, heal your inner child, and wait for the next Mercury retrograde to stop blaming you.

But the truth is: You need cash flow.

You need leverage.

You need a real business that serves real people, with real urgency.

And this is it.

🧩 The Game Plan Isn’t Complicated—But It Is Serious

Let me recap the no-BS version of your new business plan:

  • Believe in the model (or walk away now).
  • Prescreen your prospects (guard your time like it's your last dollar).
  • Run the math (talk to 30, get 15, close 5).
  • Use the tools (don't play hero—use what works).

Moonshine Capital and David Allen Capital are giving you the vehicle. Now you decide if you’re gonna get behind the wheel or keep watching from the curb.
