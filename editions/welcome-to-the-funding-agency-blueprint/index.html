Let’s get something straight.

This ain’t just another newsletter about “sales tips” or “passive income.” This is your blueprint to build a funding business that pays you like a boss — not a side hustle.

Every day, I’m unpacking the real playbook I wish I had when I first joined David Allen Capital:

  • No fluff.
  • No $997 course upsell.
  • Just clear steps to go from “I just signed up” to “I just closed $50K in funding.”

Because if you can help businesses get capital, you’ll never be broke again.

💡 Who This Is For

If you're…

  • Tired of being underpaid and underestimated.
  • A freelancer, gig worker, coach, or side hustler looking for something real.
  • Already in the DAC system but feeling stuck.
  • Or just ready to start a business that can actually change your financial life…

Then this newsletter is your unfair advantage.

📬 What You’ll Get (5 Days a Week)

Each day of the week is a targeted drop — designed to build one part of your business:

🧠 Monday – Leadership Locker Room Mindset. Tough love. Wake-up calls. Become the leader your agency needs.

👥 Tuesday – Team Building Playbook How to recruit, duplicate, and scale. Build a team that doesn’t need babysitting.

💼 Wednesday – Funding Firepower Real tactics to close deals, retain clients, and stack commissions with DAC.

💳 Thursday – Profit Tips (Payment Processing) How to double your income by stacking PayPro with your funding deals.

📣 Friday – Agent Intel & Updates Weekly updates, comp tips, scripts, DAC news — everything that keeps you sharp.

💥 Why I’m Doing This

Because most people get into DAC and still think like an employee.

They wait to be trained. They wait to be motivated. They wait for permission.

Not here.

At The Funding Agency Blueprint, we teach you how to launch fast, recruit smart, and get paid early and often.
