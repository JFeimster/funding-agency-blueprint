Stop Quoting Rates. Start Closing Deals: The January “Gym Funding” Play + What DAC Just Changed

Reviews DAC updates, broker messaging, gym-owner outreach, training, and the activity checklist presented in the January update.

Jason Feimster · Moonshine Capital

There are two kinds of brokers:

  • The ones who “sound smart” and accidentally violate compliance.
  • The ones who stay in their lane… and collect checks.

The recent Friday Broker Update was basically a friendly wake-up slap: DAC is rolling out new promos, new tools, a January-only funding special, and a March Madness-style contest ramp… but the real lesson was simpler:

Don’t present the offer. Promote the opportunity. And if you do that consistently, you become unstoppable (and legally boring, which is the best kind of boring).

Embedded content: Friday Broker Update 01/09

1) January Swag Special (Yes, This Matters More Than You Think)

DAC / Bank Breezy swag is 35% off for January. Promo code: 2026

Before you roll your eyes: swag isn’t “drip,” it’s field credibility. A hat + shirt in a local networking room signals “I’m not a random—this is what I do.”

2) Compliance Update: Don’t Quote Rates. Don’t Quote Deals. Don’t Get Cute.

A bunch of states are tightening disclosure laws. DAC already follows them internally—your job is just to not do anything dumb publicly.

Here’s the rule:

  • Do NOT quote interest rates, APR, factor rates, “annualized,” or any made-up math you saw in a Facebook comment.
  • Do NOT “present the offer” to a client (that’s the funding team’s job).
  • DO talk total cost of capital in a general way.

The cleanest language (safe + clear):

  • “If you receive $100K, you may pay back something like $115K–$150K depending on the offer.”
  • “We focus on total cost of capital and payment—simple and transparent.”

Why this matters:

If you start sounding like a lender, you can accidentally violate state rules. And worse: you can say something inaccurate and torch trust.

Be the connector. Be the closer. Don’t be the amateur underwriter.

3) New Tool: After-Hours Support Chatbot (Beta)

DAC has a 24/7 chatbot that answers most support questions:

  • override spread qualifications
  • rank / earnings questions
  • recruiting / broker basics
  • general “how does this work” stuff

It won’t answer deal-specific questions like “where’s my commission on client X?”—but for everything else, it’s a legit time-saver.

Use it. Try to break it. If it can’t answer something, report the question so they can improve the knowledge base.

Embedded content: ChatGPT

4) Agency Expansion Training (LMS): Even If You “Already Know This”

If you’re building a team, you should go through it even if you think you’re advanced, because:

  • it shows you exactly what your new brokers are being taught
  • it helps you duplicate faster
  • it reinforces the sequence (and sequence beats hype)

5) 1099s Are Coming — Don’t Self-Sabotage

If you earned $600+, you’ll get a 1099.

Two key reminders:

  • Update your address + email in the back office so your documents actually reach you.
  • If you change your email, make sure it matches the payment processor profile too—or payments can get held for verification.

Also: if you’re pursuing this as a business (even early), talk to a tax pro about Schedule C and deducting legitimate expenses. (Yes, even if your “revenue” is still $0.)

6) Decline Emails Will Improve (But Not in the Way People Want)

DAC heard the feedback: decline emails have been vague.

Update coming by end of January:

  • more useful “fixable” decline reasons (ex: excessive NSFs, prior default, etc.)
  • BUT if it’s sensitive background/character issues, it will stay “background” for privacy/legal reasons

That’s not DAC being mysterious. That’s DAC avoiding lawsuits and keeping client privacy intact.

7) The Contest Ramp Has Already Started (March Madness Incoming)

They’re already tracking:

  • applications
  • broker enrollments
  • certifications
  • fundings

And they’re showing weekly “Top 10” activity to light a fire under everyone. Translation:

The era of winning the week with one funding and calling yourself a legend is ending.

Good. Let’s raise the bar.

8) The Main Play: January Funding Special for Gyms + Fitness Businesses (January Only)

This is the money section.

DAC/Bank Breezy launched a January-only funding special for:

  • gyms
  • health clubs
  • Pilates studios
  • yoga studios
  • boxing / martial arts fitness
  • most “health + fitness” business models

The special may include:

  • lower lender fees and/or
  • lower overall cost (depending on the lender/offer)

Important detail: the incentive is tied to January fundings, not “applications submitted on January 31st.” So the move is: get gym owners in NOW, not “sometime later.”

The weekend mission:

Make a list right now:

  • every gym owner you know
  • every trainer/coach who knows an owner
  • every studio you’ve driven past 400 times and never walked into

Then hit them with a simple message:

Sample outreach script (text/DM):

“Hey — quick heads up: I’m in business funding and we have a January-only funding special specifically for gyms/fitness businesses. If you’re expanding, upgrading equipment, or just want more cash flow cushion, January is the cheapest window. Want me to send you the link to see options?”

Keep it clean. No rates. No specifics. Just the opportunity + urgency.

9) Local Leaders: You Don’t Need a City. You Need “You + One”

This part was sneaky powerful.

A “local leader” isn’t someone with an office and a blazer collection. It’s someone who can do this:

  • recruit one person
  • encourage them
  • get coffee
  • run the play together
  • duplicate to two
  • then three
  • and suddenly you’ve got momentum

Also, there was a key correction on the link:

✅ dacleader.com (singular)

They also mentioned an email-system issue that wiped a big chunk of the leaders list, so even if you think you’re subscribed, re-subscribe to be safe.

The Real Takeaway

This update wasn’t just “announcements.”

It was a blueprint:

  • stay compliant by staying simple
  • use tools to move faster
  • pick one niche with urgency (gyms)
  • recruit + certify (not just recruit)
  • and start acting like you’re building an asset, not chasing a commission

Momentum doesn’t come from motivation. It comes from a boring checklist executed aggressively.

Quick Action Checklist

  • ✅ Order swag (35% off) — code 2026
  • ✅ Stop quoting rates — only speak total cost of capital generally
  • ✅ Test the chatbot + report gaps
  • ✅ Complete Agency Expansion Training
  • ✅ Update address/email for 1099 + payouts
  • ✅ Build your “Gym Owners” list and outreach this weekend
  • ✅ Re-subscribe to the local leader list: dacleader.com
  • ✅ Prepare for Thursday night’s special event (8pm ET)
