Your Income Problem Isn’t Leads. It’s Your Identity.

Argues that a broker’s professional identity shapes follow-up, persistence, and execution more than access to additional leads.

Jason Feimster · Moonshine Capital

ORIGINAL LINKEDIN EDITION ↗
Originally published 2026-04-17. Historical offers, program terms, and results reflect the original article and may have changed. Some original illustrations are linked rather than hosted. Moonshine Capital may receive affiliate compensation; no funding or income outcome is guaranteed.

Most brokers think they have a lead problem.

They don’t.

They have an identity problem.

And until that gets fixed… you can give them 1,000 leads and they’ll still choke.

Embedded content: Self Image

🎯 The Real Game (Nobody Wants to Talk About)

In a recent training from the David Allen Capital broker channel, the message was simple—but most people will ignore it:

Your self-image determines your identity… and your identity determines your results.

Not your scripts. Not your CRM. Not your funnel.

You.

💰 Let’s Make This Practical (Not Woo-Woo BS)

Here’s what that actually looks like in the field:

Scenario:

A nail salon owner applies for $100K…

Reality says:

  • Inconsistent revenue
  • Weak months under $10K
  • Language barrier

Most brokers?

👉 Panic

👉 Ghost

👉 Or blame the lead

But the DAC system?

👉 Adjust the ask

👉 Work the deal

👉 Communicate clearly

👉 Get it done

Result: $22,000 funded in ~2 weeks.

That’s not magic. That’s identity.

👉 You either see yourself as:

  • A desperate middleman OR
  • A problem-solving capital operator

One gets ignored. The other gets paid.

🧬 The Hidden Edge: Identity > Skill

There was a study mentioned in the training:

The #1 trait of top performers wasn’t motivation, discipline, or even skill…

It was identity alignment.

Meaning:

  • They believed they were the solution
  • They acted like professionals
  • They showed up like closers

And guess what?

👉 The market responded accordingly.

⚠️ Hard Truth (You Might Not Like)

If you’re struggling right now…

It’s not because:

  • The market is bad
  • Leads are weak
  • Or the comp plan sucks

It’s because somewhere deep down…

👉 You don’t fully see yourself as “that person” yet.

And clients can feel that.

🔁 The Daily Reset (This Is the Play)

Here’s the actual “play of the day” from the training:

Make a conscious effort to see the greatness inside of you—daily.

Sounds soft… but it’s lethal if you apply it.

Translate that into broker behavior:

  • You follow up faster
  • You speak with certainty
  • You position yourself as the solution
  • You don’t fold when deals get messy

And suddenly…

👉 More apps

👉 More approvals

👉 More fundings

🧠 Tie It Back

The wild part?

The DAC system already gives you everything:

  • Proven duplication framework
  • Clear activity metrics
  • Funding pathways

Example:

👉 80 conversations ≈ 1 funded deal

👉 Scale activity = scale income

But if your identity is off?

You won’t even hit the reps needed to win.

💡 Final Thought (Read This Twice)

You don’t “become successful” in this business.

You decide who you are… and act accordingly.

Everything else follows.

⚔️ Take Action

If you’re serious about building a funding agency:

👉 Stop chasing tactics

👉 Start upgrading identity

👉 Then plug into the system

If you want the actual playbook + tools:

  • Join the #1 B2B Funding Platform
  • Learn more about starting your own agency
  • 10 Plug-and-Play Automations (Scale Your Agency)

You know where to find me.
