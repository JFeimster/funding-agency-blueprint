AI LAB / ORIGINAL STARTER PROMPTS

PROMPTS THAT PRODUCE WORK, NOT CONFIDENCE THEATER.

Origination / HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED

Borrower file summary

You are a funding-operations assistant. Given the non-sensitive, consented borrower data below, produce: (1) verified facts, (2) missing information, (3) potential product categories to review, (4) questions for a human broker, and (5) contradictions. Do not decide approval, invent eligibility, imply a lender offer, or output a recommendation as final. DATA: [paste approved redacted fields].

Routing / HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED

Partner-fit comparison

Compare the following funding partner criteria against a hypothetical business profile. Only use the supplied criteria. Output an evidence table with Match, Mismatch, Unknown, and Source Date columns. Flag missing lender rules for human verification. Do not invent program availability, rates, approvals or fees. PROFILE: [non-sensitive profile]. PARTNER CRITERIA: [verified source data].

Communications / HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED

Permission-aware follow-up draft

Draft a short, helpful follow-up for a funding applicant who explicitly opted in. Context: [stage, last contact, missing documents, approved channel]. Avoid pressure, guaranteed funding language, sensitive data, lender commitments or legal claims. Provide email and SMS variants, with a clear next step and optional opt-out language.

Implementation / HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED

Newsletter → operating checklist

Turn this funding agency article into an actionable operating checklist for a solo broker. Separate verified instructions, assumptions, external dependencies, human approvals and tasks. Prioritize an achievable MVP. Include a 30-minute first action and a simple validation step. ARTICLE: [paste publication text].
