### The “25 Active Brokers” Vision (and why it’s not fantasy)

Close your eyes and run this scenario:

- You have **25 active brokers**
- Your team produces **5 fundings per week total** (not each… total)
- You average **$500 per funding in override**

That’s **$2,500/week**.

Now do the boring math: $2,500 × 52 weeks = **$130,000/year**.

That’s six figures in overrides from a team that isn’t even “huge.”

> **Important adult disclaimer:** those numbers are illustrative, not a promise. Results vary. No one is guaranteed anything. But the math shows what’s possible *when structure exists.*

Now here’s the punchline:

That vision becomes useless the moment you stop at “wow, that’d be nice.”

So let’s do the part most people won’t do.

### Work backwards like a grown-up

If you want **25 active**, you probably need more than 25 enrolled.

Why?

Because in commission-based, agent-based businesses, **most people don’t stick**. Not because they’re incapable—because they don’t choose the work long enough.

So the structure question becomes:

- **How many enrolled brokers does it take to end the year with 25 active?**
- What do you need to do differently so more of them actually *activate*?

This is where structure stops being “theory” and becomes your paycheck.

## The 3 Plays (aka: homework you can’t outsource)

### Play #1: Reality check

**How many active brokers do I have right now?**

Not “how many signed up.” Active.

### Play #2: Leadership check

**Am I willing to support a team like a real leader?**

Not “I enrolled you, good luck.” Support.

### Play #3: Calendar check

**Am I clearing my schedule to get the structure and run it?**

Because nothing says “I want success” like never showing up.

### The gym membership analogy (painfully accurate)

Selling someone a gym membership doesn’t get them fit.

But what happens if you sell them a membership *and*:

- Give them a plan
- Check in regularly
- Pair them with a workout buddy
- Encourage them
- Lead by example
- Run incentives
- Actually train with them

More people succeed. Obviously.

That’s the point:

**If you treat your brokers like “memberships,” they’ll behave like memberships.** If you train them like athletes, they’ll produce like athletes.

## Your structure beats your personality

You don’t need to be charismatic. You don’t need to be “a closer.” You don’t need to be an extrovert in a blazer.

You need a repeatable onboarding + activity structure.

Here’s a simple version you can run without becoming a corporate robot:

## Broker Activation Structure (simple + ruthless)

### Day 1 (same day they enroll):

- Welcome message + next 3 steps
- Book a 15-min “Game Plan” call

### Within 48 hours:

- Get them into certification / LMS
- Set activity target for the week (small, specific)

### Weekly cadence:

- 1 quick check-in (text/DM)
- 1 accountability touch (numbers, not vibes)
- 1 “win story” share (social proof)

### The Game Plan questions (burn these into memory):

- What’s your goal?
- What’s your reality?
- What accounts for the difference?
- What can be learned?

That’s not just “nice coaching.” That’s how you turn “enrolled” into “active.”

## The uncomfortable truth: you don’t need 1,000 people

You need **a few people supported well**… and duplication.

The huddle made this point clean:

If you can support **5 or 10** properly, they duplicate, and suddenly you’ve got multiple local markets moving.

The fantasy is chasing “more people.” The real game is building **a structure that activates people.**
