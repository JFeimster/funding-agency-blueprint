## 20 In-Depth FAQs

- **What is DAC and how does it help entrepreneurs?** [DAC (David Allen Capital)](https://davidallencapital.com/jason) is a business funding platform that provides capital to small businesses often overlooked by banks. Entrepreneurs can earn commissions by helping businesses get fast funding.
- **What’s the “preferred product incentive” mentioned in the update?** It’s a promotion where agents earn 10% more commission on qualified fundings using [DAC’s preferred funding options](https://davidallencapital.com/select-funding/jason/)—those offering steep prepayment discounts.
- **How fast can someone get funding through DAC?** In many cases, approved clients can receive funding within 24-48 hours. Some have been approved and funded within a single business day.
- **What are the qualifications for preferred funding?** [Preferred funding](https://davidallencapital.com/select-funding/jason/) typically requires: $30K/month revenue (lowest of last 4 months), 680+ FICO score, 2+ years in business. Higher terms need 100K/month and 700+ FICO.
- **What’s a 100% prepayment discount?** If a client pays off their advance early, they owe only the unpaid principal—completely waiving the remaining interest. It’s rare and a massive value-add.
- **How can new agents get started quickly?** By focusing on warm market outreach, local business outreach, and consistent social media sharing. Activity is the fastest path to results.
- **Is certification required to be paid?** You’ll still get paid without certification, but much less. Certification is required for full commissions and to be considered a “qualified agent.”
- **Can someone work this as a side hustle?** Absolutely. Many agents are gig workers, freelancers, and side hustlers who build [part-time DAC agencies into full-time incomes](https://davidallencapital.com/partner/jason/).
- **What are the main industries DAC targets?** Construction, trades, landscaping, home healthcare, retail, restaurants, and manufacturing are top-performing verticals.
- **How do I follow up with a stalled application?** If an applicant doesn’t complete their submission, follow up with a personal call or message to assist them. Many deals are salvaged this way.
- **What makes DAC different from banks?** DAC funds businesses that banks often deny. There’s no requirement for collateral, and approvals are faster with fewer hoops.
- **How does DAC pay agents?** Agents are paid commissions per funded deal, with overrides on agents they personally recruit, plus promotional bonuses.
- **How big can an agency get?** There’s no cap. Some agents build large organizations earning overrides on dozens (or hundreds) of active funding agents.
- **What are micro-sharing points?** It’s part of a daily activity tracker where you earn points by taking daily actions—like calls, messages, videos—and share your progress.
- **Can clients use the funds for anything?** Yes. [Working capital from DAC](https://davidallencapital.com/business-capital/jason/) can be used for payroll, expansion, equipment, renovations, or debt consolidation.
- **Do I need to be a financial expert to join DAC?** Nope. You just need the ability to talk to people, follow training, and take action. The platform and training do the heavy lifting.
- **What if a business doesn’t meet preferred criteria?** They may still qualify for other DAC programs. Agents are encouraged to submit every lead—even if they don’t hit “perfect” numbers.
- **What’s the role of social media in this business?** It’s your digital storefront. Sharing DAC content (and adding your link) builds visibility, trust, and attracts both clients and agents.
- **Are there live trainings I can join?** Yes. There are daily Zoom trainings at 11:30 AM ET, plus recordings for those who can’t attend live. These keep you sharp and informed.
- **Can I build recurring income with DAC?** Absolutely. By recruiting and developing agents, you earn override commissions for the lifetime of their business activity
