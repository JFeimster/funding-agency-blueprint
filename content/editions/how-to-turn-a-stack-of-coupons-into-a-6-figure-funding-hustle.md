## 📞 Follow-Up Isn’t Optional. It’s the Job.

Wade pulled back the curtain on what really happens after a client fills out the Bank Breezy application:

- They (and you) get email confirmations.
- If they stall out mid-app, the team *calls* them.
- If they can’t upload bank statements, someone walks them through it.
- Once offers come in, **you’re notified immediately**.

**What’s your job?** Follow up. Text them. Call them. Reaffirm that *someone's working on their behalf*. Don’t leave them dangling like every other scammy online lender.

Your lead isn’t just “waiting to hear back.” They’re being actively supported by a whole backend team—*but it’s your job* to stay plugged in and encourage completion.
